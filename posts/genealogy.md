The motivation behind doing so is rather selfish, in that I want to learn about this and understand why, this is not actively meant as an end-all resource. Also as I imagine as I delve further in this process, I will veer to topics that are adjacent but not at the core of language models; moreso, technqiues employed for SOTA models to make them better.
